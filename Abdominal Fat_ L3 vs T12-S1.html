Abdominal fat: AbdoCompL3 vs T12-S1 DIXON VIBE

How well the two abdominal sequences agree on SAT and VAT where they cover the same body region. Report date 11 Oct 2026. Cases are labelled A1–A5.

Summary

  • SAT agrees between the sequences within 1% in every case (mean absolute difference 0.4%).
  • VAT differs more: T12 minus L3 is −0.2% to +6.4% (mean +2.2%). T12 measures more VAT in 4 of 5 cases.
  • Total fat (SAT + VAT) agrees within 1.8%.
  • In the case with the largest VAT difference (A4), raising the T12 VAT Fat Fraction from 0.40 to 0.42 equalises the volumes, but pixel overlap does not improve (VAT Dice 0.756 at both). The remaining disagreement is in where thin VAT strands are found, not in how much VAT there is.

Data and method

  • Sequences: the fat image (_F) of AbdoCompL3 and of T12-S1 DIXON VIBE, scanned in the same session. Example geometry (A4): L3 512 × 512 pixels of 0.98 mm with 3 mm slices; T12 260 × 320 pixels of 1.375 mm with 4 mm slices.
  • Segmentation: fatSeg's automatic abdominal method (abd_seg.py), no hand edits. The fat signal of each slice is the median SAT brightness, smoothed over neighbouring slices. SAT and the SAT/VAT boundary use 40% of that signal. The boundary combines rays from the body centre with a closed boundary path, and is checked against neighbouring slices. A pixel inside the boundary is VAT when it reaches the VAT Fat Fraction (default 0.40) of the fat signal.
  • Shared region: each scan is cut to the slab the other scan covers, along that scan's own slice direction (120 mm per case). In one case (A2) the two scans are tilted 3.9° against each other; the cut accounts for it.
  • Comparison: volumes in cm³; differences are T12 − L3 as a percentage of the mean of the two. Pixel overlap (Dice: 1 = identical, 0 = no shared pixels) was measured by sampling T12 labels at L3 voxel centres from the DICOM geometry, without registration.

  • The volumes match at about 0.42 (+1.2%), so the original gap corresponds to a change of only 0.025 in the setting.
  • Pixel overlap stays at VAT Dice 0.75–0.76 at every value. About 21–23% of L3 VAT pixels fall on T12 background and about 1% on T12 SAT. The SAT/VAT boundary itself agrees well (SAT Dice 0.955).
  • VAT Dice per slice is 0.67–0.82 on all 40 L3 slices, so the mismatch is spread evenly rather than confined to a few slices.

Interpretation

SAT is a large, compact region and both sequences measure it almost identically. VAT is largely made of thin strands between bowel loops. The two sequences sample them differently (0.98 vs 1.375 mm pixels, 3 vs 4 mm slices), and the bowel can shift between breath-holds, so a strand displaced by 1–2 mm lands on background in the other scan. This explanation has not been verified directly.

Tuning the VAT Fat Fraction per scan can equalise VAT volumes, but it does not make the segmentations agree pixel by pixel, and nothing independent shows that a different value is more correct for T12. The tendency of T12 to show slightly more VAT (4 of 5 cases) needs more cases before the default is changed.
